**Title:** Stub: event tickets paid in NIM, checked in on-chain (Cycle III, Pay)

Hi everyone 👋 I'm Tolu, a graphic designer from Lagos. For Cycle III I built **Stub**, a ticketing mini app for small events.

Hosts create an event and share a link or a poster. Guests pay in NIM (or USDT) inside Nimiq Pay, and the payment, with a `STUB:<event>:<qty>` memo, *is* the ticket. At the door you scan the QR, and Stub verifies it on-chain and admits each ticket once. There's no backend and no custody; the event lives in the link.
